Monthly Parking Terms

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains exactly how your City Park Management monthly parking pass works — billing, cancellation, plate changes, and what happens if a payment fails. These terms apply in addition to our general Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

How Billing Works

Your monthly pass is billed automatically to the card on file. Your first payment is prorated based on how many days remain in the current month, so you only pay for the days you'll actually use before your first full monthly charge.

Starting the following month, your card is charged the full monthly rate on the 1st of each month, and continues automatically until you cancel.

If your lot has a service fee, it is included in every charge — both your first prorated payment and every recurring monthly charge afterward.

Managing Your Pass

You can manage your pass at any time at cityparkmanagement.app/manage-pass. Enter the email your pass is registered under, and you'll receive a secure link (valid for 30 minutes) to:

  • Update your email address
  • Update your license plate
  • Update your card on file
  • Cancel your pass

License Plate Changes

You may update your license plate up to 2 times per month. This limit resets at the start of each calendar month. Saving the same plate again does not count against this limit.

Your license plate is your parking pass — there is no physical permit. Make sure the plate on file always matches your vehicle, since enforcement staff verify passes by plate.

Cancellation

You may cancel your pass at any time through the manage-pass link above. Canceling stops all future billing, but you'll retain parking access through the end of your current paid period — we do not provide partial refunds for the remainder of a billing period.

If a Payment Fails

If we're unable to process your monthly charge (an expired card, insufficient funds, etc.), your pass is marked past due and you'll receive an email with a link to update your payment method.

You have a 48-hour grace period from the time of the failed payment to update your card. During this window, your pass generally remains valid, though it may show as "at risk" to enforcement staff. Updating your card during this window will automatically retry the failed charge.

If payment is not resolved within 48 hours, your pass is automatically canceled and you'll receive an email confirming this. You would need to sign up again to restore parking access.

No Guaranteed Space

A monthly pass provides authorization to park at your assigned lot — it does not guarantee an available, reserved, or specific parking space. On busy days, all spaces in the lot may be full even for authorized monthly parkers. City Park Management and the property owner are not responsible for lost time, inconvenience, or any other consequence of a full lot.

Access Restrictions

Some lots restrict monthly pass access to specific days or hours (for example, weekdays only, or business hours only). If your lot has restrictions, they'll be shown to you at signup and on your pass details. Parking outside your permitted hours may result in a violation.

Enrollment

Monthly passes are available either by direct signup at participating lots, or by invitation from City Park Management or the property owner. Availability may be limited by the lot's monthly pass capacity.

Questions

If you have questions about your monthly pass, billing, or need help outside of what's available at the manage-pass page, contact us at info@cityparkmanagement.com.
